# Stock logo sources

Company marks are bundled so stock cards render without third-party image requests or broken-image fallbacks.

- The original Daybreak SVG set remains under this directory.
- PNG marks added for the 2026-09-30 Base catalog expansion came from the public [ticker-logos collection](https://github.com/psrijan/ticker-logos).
- Reddit uses the Reddit mark from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/?q=reddit), which links to Reddit's official brand guidance.
- Trump Media and the current SanDisk wordmark came from their Wikimedia Commons logo files.

All company names and marks remain the property of their respective owners and are used for identification.
